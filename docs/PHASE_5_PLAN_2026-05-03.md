## Failure modes to watch for

1. **136939 fails or times out at the 24h soft ceiling.** Diagnose via `condor_history 136939` and the per-task TSV files — partial data is recoverable. If most tasks completed, run Steps 1-5 with what's there and footnote the missing cells.
2. **A new comparator silently produces no output** (the BioConvert T4 pattern from this session). Step 1's eyeball pass should catch it. If not, Step 2 will flag it as suspicious. Refuse to ship until every cell has a verdict or an explicit "skip with reason."
3. **The new data overturns a locked framing decision.** Pause the chain. Document the contradiction in `FRAMING_DECISIONS_2026-05-03.md` as a new section. Decide whether to revise the framing (which restarts manuscript drafting) or annotate the contradiction in the manuscript Limitations section.
4. **Statistical-genetics or brutal-critic surfaces a methodology bug.** Address before Step 5; manuscript prose is downstream of stats correctness.
